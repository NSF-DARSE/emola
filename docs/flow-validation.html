Two things worth knowing

Abnormal events are about 1% of the mailbox. Seven notices hidden in 603 is a strong argument that they are easy to miss — and the clearest reason to automate the sorting rather than the writing.

One subject line reads “Scheduled IRAS Outage — Sunday, August 2, 2926”. A thousand-year typo in live mail. We are keeping it as the example of why a person still approves anything that goes out.
